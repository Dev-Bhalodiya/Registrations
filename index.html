How dates work here

Enter dates as DD/MM/YYYY (e.g. 15/03/2026) or YYYY-MM-DD (e.g. 2026-03-15). A date typed the U.S. way, like 03/15/2026, will be rejected rather than silently misread — that's deliberate.

Uploading a CSV or Excel file? Format the date columns as one of the patterns above before saving. If a column got auto-converted into a plain number (like 46095), that's fine too — Excel/Sheets date serial numbers are recognised automatically.

Manual entry uses your browser's own date picker, so the format question never comes up there.

Anything downloaded from this page comes out as YYYY-MM-DD, which Excel and Google Sheets both read as a real date automatically — not as text.
